Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you travel outside Amravati for weddings?
Yes! While we are based in Amravati, we regularly travel across all of Maharashtra (Nagpur, Akola, Yavatmal, Wardha, Chandrapur, Pune, Mumbai) as well as destination weddings all over India. Travel and stay expenses are discussed simply in advance.
How do I confirm my wedding date?
Dates are confirmed strictly upon paying an advance booking deposit. Since wedding season gets booked very fast, we recommend confirming your date 1 to 4 months in advance.
Is saree draping and hairstyle included in the bridal package?
Yes! Our bridal package includes everything: makeup, bridal hairstyle with fresh flowers, jewelry setting, eyelashes, and complete saree or lehenga draping. You don't need to hire anyone else.
Will the makeup suit my skin tone?
Yes! We match the foundation shade exactly to your natural skin tone. We believe in enhancing your natural beauty so you look radiant and confident, not artificial or white.
