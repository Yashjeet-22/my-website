Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book my wedding date?
Due to high demand during peak wedding seasons (October to May), we recommend reserving your date at least 2 to 6 months in advance. Dates are reserved strictly upon receipt of an advance booking deposit.
Do you travel to venues and destination weddings?
Yes! We provide on-location services across Pune, Mumbai, Maharashtra, and destination wedding locations throughout India. Travel and accommodation logistics are arranged in accordance with client convenience.
Which international makeup brands do you use?
We maintain an uncompromising luxury cosmetic kit featuring Charlotte Tilbury, M·A·C Pro, Dior Beauty, Estée Lauder, NARS, Bobbi Brown, Huda Beauty, and Kryolan. Everything is dermatologically tested, hygienic, and long-wearing.
Do you offer paid pre-bridal trials?
Yes, we offer customized bridal trials where we explore makeup shades, base textures, and hairstyle concepts to finalize your signature look before your wedding festivities begin.
Does your bridal package include draping and jewelry setting?
Absolutely! Complete styling assistance—including heavy lehenga draping, Nauvari pleating, double dupatta securing, matha patti/maang tikka alignment, and jewelry pinning—is fully included in our bridal packages.
